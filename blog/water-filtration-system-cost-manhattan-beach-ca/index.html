Water Filtration System Cost in Manhattan Beach, CA: Options and Upkeep

The right system depends on what you want out of the water: better taste, less scale, or both. A softener and a filter are not the same machine, and mixing them up is how people overpay.

We start with your tap and the City's numbers, then match the equipment to the problem.

By the Manhattan Beach 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated October 15, 2026.

What a water filtration system costs depends on the job

A water filtration system is any equipment that treats water after it enters the building, from a pitcher-sized cartridge to a whole-home tank. Cost depends on three things: how much water is treated, what is removed, and how much upkeep you accept.

People often ask for one price. In practice there are four separate products and each has its own cost curve. Point-of-use filters treat one tap, reverse osmosis treats one tap more deeply, whole-home filters treat everything at the entry, and softeners deal with hardness only.

The cheapest approach is to treat only the water you drink. The most thorough approach treats all of it and protects the plumbing.

Cost drivers that change the total

Ask for these items by name when you collect quotes.

  • Capacity and flow rate.

    A softener is sized by grains of capacity and by peak flow in gpm. Bathrooms, family size and hardness all push capacity up.

  • Location and plumbing changes.

    Where the unit goes, whether a drain and power outlet are nearby, and whether the main line needs new valves or a bypass all affect labor.

  • Pressure and protection.

    Some houses need a PRV check or a pressure gauge first, since high pressure shortens the life of tanks and fittings.

  • Drain connection and brine disposal.

    A softener needs an air gap to a drain. A beach lot with limited utility room space can mean a tighter install.

  • Brand, media and warranty terms.

    Resin quality, valve design and electronics vary. Ask what the warranty covers and who services it.

Manhattan Beach note

What the City's 2023 water report says about your tap

The City of Manhattan Beach reported that its tap water met all EPA and state primary drinking water standards in the 2023 Annual Water Quality Report. It also reported lead at the tap with a 90th percentile of 4.4 micrograms per liter across 33 samples in 2022, and copper with a 90th percentile of 0.29 mg/L.

The report says both lead and copper come mainly from corrosion of household plumbing, and that the City cannot control plumbing materials inside your home. For that reason it advises flushing the tap for 30 seconds to 2 minutes when water has sat for several hours. The same report notes a disinfectant residual of 1.8 mg/L average as chlorine/chloramine, which is what carbon filters mainly target.

If you are in Hermosa Beach or Redondo Beach, check which system serves your address, since Cal Water's Hermosa-Redondo district publishes its own report.

Sources: City of Manhattan Beach water quality reports

Test your tap before you buy

Equipment should match a measurement, not a sales pitch.

  1. 1

    Read the City's annual report

    It lists hardness, pH, disinfectant and the lead and copper results for the whole system.

  2. 2

    Test at your own faucet

    Hardness strips are cheap, but a lab test for lead and copper at your tap tells you about your house plumbing. Test first-draw and after flushing.

  3. 3

    Match the complaint to the equipment

    Taste goes to carbon or RO. Scale goes to a softener. Lead worries go to a certified filter, plus flushing.

  4. 4

    Get an itemized quote

    Compare equipment, install labor, permits if applicable and first fill. Our water softener and filtration work follows that order, and a repiping conversation is separate if the pipes themselves are the source.
