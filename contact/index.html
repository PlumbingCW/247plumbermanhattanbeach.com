What to have ready when you call

A few details speed everything up. None of them are required, and you can fill in gaps as we talk.

Where you are

Give the street address and cross street or landmark. Mention whether it is a house, condo, apartment or business, and whether the shutoff is shared with neighbors.

What you see and hear

Say where the water is, whether it is clear or dirty, and whether it is still running. For drains, say which fixtures are slow and which are fine. For a water heater, say whether it is gas or electric.

What you have tried

Tell us if you closed the main, reset a breaker, used a plunger or poured anything down the drain. It stops us repeating steps and helps us avoid advising something unsafe.

If water is flowing right now

Do not wait to call before acting. The first minute matters more than the phone call, and the steps below stop damage while you reach us.

If you smell gas, skip all of this. Leave on foot, call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe distance, or 911 if life or property could be in danger. Do not touch switches or use a phone inside.

Before you dial

  1. Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. Turn off power to wet rooms at the breaker if water is near outlets or appliances.
  3. Open the lowest faucet to drain pressure from the lines.
  4. Move rugs, electronics and valuables away from the water.

Calling about a rental, condo or business

Landlords, tenants and property managers call us often, and a little prep avoids confusion. Tell the dispatcher who is authorizing the work. A tenant can report the problem, but the person who approves the price may be the owner or the management company.

In multifamily buildings, say whether the line serves several units. A clog in a shared stack in a building near Manhattan Village or Sepulveda Blvd can back up into a ground-floor unit, so the plumber may need access to a cleanout or the roof vent.

For shops and offices, mention opening hours. We can often plan the visit around staff and customers.
