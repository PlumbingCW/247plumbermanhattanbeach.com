About the area

What East Manhattan Beach covers

Wikipedia lists East Manhattan Beach as including Manhattan Village, Manhattan Heights and Liberty Village. Aviation Boulevard is the eastern edge of the city. Real estate guides center the Heights and Liberty Village area on Polliwog Park, with Manhattan Heights running from Sepulveda to Redondo Avenue and Liberty Village from Redondo Avenue to Aviation, between Marine Avenue and Manhattan Beach Boulevard.

City-Data's estimates give East Manhattan Beach about 15,100 residents, Manhattan Heights about 3,950 and Liberty Village about 3,040. Those are rough figures from boundary data that is more than ten years old.

Two housing stories in one part of town

A real estate guide says Liberty Village has nearly identical two and three bedroom post-war cottages from about 1950, and that many have been preserved, remodeled or expanded. The same guide describes single-story Roth Built homes in Manhattan Heights from the mid-1960s, with lots near 5,000 square feet close to Sepulveda and 7,000 to 7,500 square feet farther east.

We do not read anything into those dates about the pipes. When a cottage is expanded, new drains and supply lines tie into what is already there, so a camera look and a pressure check tell you more than the year does. New work is judged against the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the City's permit process.

Address tip

ZIP codes in East Manhattan Beach can mislead

City-Data lists ZIP codes 90266 and 90278 for Liberty Village. The 90278 ZIP is also Redondo Beach's, but Liberty Village is inside Manhattan Beach. A shared ZIP does not put two places in the same city, so we go by the street address.

The City asks owners to call before flushing or cleaning a sewer lateral, with 24 to 48 hours of notice, because extra flow can overload the main. The lateral is the owner's responsibility under Manhattan Beach Municipal Code 5.36.240. If your address sits near Redondo Beach or El Segundo, see plumbing in Redondo Beach or El Segundo.
