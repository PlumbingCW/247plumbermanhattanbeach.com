A city where a plumbing problem is often a business problem

An emergency plumber in El Segundo, CA usually gets the call from someone with a deadline. The city has LAX immediately to its north and a Chevron refinery that covers more than a quarter of its land, and the city also holds offices, startups and a residential side. A burst riser or a backed-up floor drain at 11 p.m. stops work the next morning, which is why emergency dispatch available around the clock matters here.

El Segundo is also a small place. The 2020 census counted 17,272 residents. Manhattan Beach is its southern neighbor, so a crew from our office on North Sepulveda Boulevard has a short run up. The address you give us decides everything, so have the street and suite ready when you call.

El Segundo projects are built to the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and commercial tenants should ask the city which approvals a change needs.

Three El Segundo districts, three ways to call us

The city splits into areas with different buildings and different priorities.

Smoky Hollow

A former commercial area now home to artists and tech and defense startups. In converted buildings, supply lines and drains can cross between suites, so we trace where each line runs before cutting. Tell us if your suite shares a restroom or a supply line with another tenant.

The eastern industrial and office district

Aviation Boulevard roughly marks the eastern boundary, and the Metro K Line runs through the district with a station at Douglas. Larger buildings here have their own risers and floor drains, and an after-hours leak often means finding the building engineer first.

West of Pacific Coast Highway

This is the city's residential side, with a beach of its own. Calls here look like any coastal home: water heaters, slow kitchen lines, supply stops that will not close. Salt air is hard on exposed valves.

How an after-hours commercial call runs

A call from a property manager or business owner follows four steps.

  1. 1

    Tell us the building

    Give the street, the suite and what the water is doing. Say whether anyone is on site who can open a mechanical room or a roof hatch.

  2. 2

    Isolate the water

    If the leak can be stopped at a suite shutoff, we talk you through it. Otherwise we find the building's isolation valve on arrival.

  3. 3

    Find the cause

    Camera on the drain line, moisture meter on the ceiling and pressure test on the supply. We show you what we found before we touch anything.

  4. 4

    Price, then repair

    You hear a clear price before work starts. Repairs that need parts we do not carry are booked as same-day appointments when available.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

These steps apply to a suite, a shop or a house west of PCH.

Shut off the main valve

For a house, look at the front of the lot or where the line enters the building. In a commercial space, the valve may be in a mechanical room or at the building entry, so call the building manager for access if needed.

Kill power and protect equipment

Switch off breakers to wet areas and unplug equipment off the floor. Computers, label printers and shop machinery are the usual losses in a flood. Move them up before you call if it is safe.

Call the 24/7 line

Have the street address, suite and the best phone number for whoever can open the space. If the building has a security desk, tell them to expect a plumber.

El Segundo water and sewer: what to ask

We have not confirmed which utility supplies each El Segundo address, so we give no hardness figure here. Request the annual water quality report from your provider and read it for total hardness, the disinfectant and the source. West Basin Municipal Water District, whose territory includes El Segundo, delivers imported MWD water to its member agencies.

For sewer rules, ask El Segundo public works who maintains the main and where the owner's lateral begins. Hard water or not, a water heater and a floor drain are the two commercial fixtures that fail most often after hours.
