Redondo Beach is bigger than it looks from the pier

Looking for an emergency plumber in Redondo Beach, CA means working across a lot of ground. The city covers 6.21 square miles, more than Manhattan Beach (about 3.94) and Hermosa Beach (about 1.42) put together, and 71,576 people lived here at the 2020 census. Our emergency dispatch available around the clock treats every address in that area the same way.

The size matters on a call. A cracked supply line in a North Redondo house, a flooded storeroom on the International Boardwalk and a dead water heater in a Riviera Village condo need different tools and different first questions. We ask where you are, what kind of building it is and what the water is doing, then send the right truck.

In Redondo Beach the same California Plumbing Code applies, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and permit questions go to the city's building division.

Three parts of town, three kinds of call

Redondo does not behave like one neighborhood. Here is how the calls tend to split.

North Redondo Beach

Residential calls dominate the inland side of town. The usual ones are water heaters that stop heating, main drains that slow down over a week and a toilet that will not stop running. For a whole-house slow drain, we run a drain camera down the lateral before choosing a cable or a hydro-jet nozzle.

Riviera Village and South Redondo

Where businesses and homes sit close together, one leak can wet a neighbor. In any stacked or shared building, the first job is finding which unit shares the supply line and closing its isolation valve. Tell us if you share a wall or ceiling with anyone.

King Harbor, the pier and the boardwalk

Properties by the water deal with salt air on valves and fittings, floor drains in food-service spaces and restrooms that carry heavy traffic. Our commercial plumbing crews handle after-hours calls on these sites.

Not sure which ZIP you are in? You do not need to know. Read us the street address instead.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

Done in this order, these three steps hold the damage while we drive over.

Shut off the main valve

Look near the water meter at the front of the property or at the wall where the line enters the building. In a condo, close the unit shutoff first. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Kill power and clear people

Cut breakers to wet rooms and keep everyone out of standing water. Shut off the water heater at its own switch or gas valve. In a shop or restaurant, clear the floor of staff before anything else.

Call the 24/7 line

Have the street address, the ZIP, whether it is a house or a suite and the spot where water is showing. Photos of the leak help if you can take them safely.

Redondo Beach note

Redondo Beach water: same Cal Water district as Hermosa

Cal Water's Hermosa-Redondo district serves Redondo Beach, along with Hermosa Beach and portions of Torrance. Two local wells feed it, and the utility buys the rest as surface water from MWD. Cal Water treats the well water for iron and manganese and blends it to lower the dissolved solids that seawater intrusion adds.

Check which system serves your address. Cal Water only serves portions of some areas, and your bill names the utility. Sewer lateral rules belong to the city, so ask Redondo public works.

Sources: Cal Water
