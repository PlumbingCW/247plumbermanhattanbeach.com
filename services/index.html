How we choose between repair and replacement

A repair is usually right when the fault is local, such as a worn flapper, a failed supply stop or a single pinhole in copper. Replacement makes sense when the same part keeps failing or when the equipment is at the end of its life, like a tank that is leaking through the shell.

We lay out both options with the price of each before we start, so you can compare. Hydro jetting, for example, clears grease and scale from a line but cannot fix a collapsed pipe, which is where a camera and a repair come in.

Why local conditions matter

Manhattan Beach water is a blend of groundwater and imported surface water, and the City's 2023 Annual Water Quality Report puts groundwater hardness at an average of 392 mg/L. Hard water shortens the life of heating elements and tankless exchangers, which is why a tankless water heater often needs descaling.

Salt air near The Strand corrodes outdoor valves and fittings, and hillside homes can see higher pressure at the bottom of the slope. We take these things into account when we recommend parts.
